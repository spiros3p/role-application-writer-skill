# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want participation in this project to be a harassment-free experience for
everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability,
ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of
experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance,
race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Behaviour that builds this environment:

- Being welcoming to newcomers, including those whose first patch is small
- Accepting constructive criticism gracefully, and giving it kindly
- Focusing on what is best for the project and for the people using it
- Assuming good faith, particularly when discussing someone else's writing
- Remembering that a rejected proposal is not a rejected person

Behaviour that does not:

- Sexualised language or imagery, and sexual attention or advances of any kind
- Trolling, insults, derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks
- Public or private harassment
- Publishing others' private information without their explicit permission
- Sustained disruption of discussion, or dismissing others' contributions on
  grounds of who they are rather than what they said

## Scope

This applies in all project spaces: the repository, issues, pull requests,
discussions, and any release or review comment. It also applies when someone is
representing the project in public.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behaviour through GitHub's private reporting on this
repository, or by contacting the maintainer directly through the address listed
on their GitHub profile.

Reports are reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly. The maintainer will
respect the privacy and security of anyone who reports an incident.

Maintainers who do not follow or enforce this code in good faith may face
temporary or permanent repercussions as determined by other maintainers.
